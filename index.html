The Lead Leak

Every Missed Call Is a Potential Job.

When storms hit or roofs start leaking, homeowners contact multiple contractors at once. The first company to respond and book the inspection almost always wins the contract.

Calls Missed While on a Roof

When your team is on a tear-off, measuring slopes, or meeting adjusters, incoming calls go straight to voicemail. Homeowners rarely leave messages—they simply call the next contractor.

Result: Lost high-value jobs to faster competitors

Homeowners Contacting Multiple Contractors

Prospects search online and submit quote requests across several companies in minutes. If you wait an hour to reply, another contractor has already secured the inspection.

Result: Zero opportunity to present your estimate

Estimates Never Followed Up

Estimators deliver detailed quotes, then get busy measuring new jobs. Without automated follow-up sequences, warm opportunities quietly slip away and go cold.

Result: Wasted drive time & lost closed deals

Storm Surges Overwhelm Your Office

When severe hail or wind strikes, lead volume spikes dramatically overnight. Busy phone lines cause high-value insurance restoration claims to get lost in the noise.

Result: Overwhelmed office staff & missed insurance claims

Expensive Ad Leads Going Cold

You spend good money on Google and social ads. When leads arrive after hours or on weekends and wait hours for a reply, that marketing budget is burned.

Result: Wasted marketing spend & rising acquisition cost

Opportunities Lost in the Shuffle

Managing leads across text messages, emails, and notepads leads to missed appointments, forgotten callbacks, and zero visibility into your upcoming revenue pipeline.

Result: Chaotic scheduling & unpredictable cash flow

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Faine Labs and our automated revenue workflows.

Who is Faine Labs built for?

Faine Labs is built exclusively for roofing companies that want to automate lead response, follow-up, qualification, and appointment booking without adding extra manual work for their team.

What happens when we miss a call while on a job?

Within 3 seconds of a missed call, the system sends a personalized SMS to the caller (e.g., "Hi! Sorry we missed your call—we're currently on a job. How can we help you today?"). This engages the homeowner immediately before they call another contractor.

How does automated follow-up work for estimate requests?

If a prospect receives an estimate or submits an inquiry but doesn't book immediately, our automated SMS and email sequences follow up politely at timed intervals. As soon as the homeowner responds, the automation pauses automatically so your team can step in.

Can prospects schedule inspections directly into our calendar?

Yes. Qualified leads can choose from open time slots that sync in real-time with Google Calendar or Outlook. Automated confirmation texts and day-of reminders are sent to ensure homeowners are ready when your estimator arrives.

Does this support storm damage and insurance restoration inquiries?

Yes. The AI is trained to recognize hail, wind, and storm damage scenarios, collecting critical details (such as date of storm, insurance carrier, leak locations, and emergency tarping requirements) before scheduling the inspection.

Can this integrate with our current workflow?

Yes. Faine Labs can operate as your complete lead management hub or seamlessly connect with your existing website forms, ads, and calendar dispatch processes without disrupting how your team currently works.

How quickly can we get started?

Most systems are fully configured, custom-trained, and live within 3 to 5 business days. We handle the entire technical setup so your team can focus on running inspections and closing contracts.
