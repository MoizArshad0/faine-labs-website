Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Faine Labs and how our lead automation system works.

What exactly does the Faine Labs automation system do?

Faine Labs installs a complete lead response and pipeline automation system. When a prospect reaches out—via phone call, website form, or chat—the system responds in seconds, answers questions, qualifies their needs, follows up persistently, and guides them to book an appointment on your calendar.

Who is this system best suited for?

Our system is built specifically for service businesses where every lead matters—such as roofing contractors, home services, HVAC, plumbing, med spas, dental clinics, real estate, and professional services. If missed calls or delayed responses cost your business money, this system is designed to solve that problem.

How does the Missed-Call Recovery feature work?

When someone calls your business and the call is missed, our system immediately sends a personalized text message to the caller in under 3 seconds (e.g., *"Hi! Sorry we missed your call, how can we help you?"*). This engages the caller before they move on to call a competitor.

How does the AI qualify leads and handle questions?

We train the AI on your specific business details: your services, pricing guidelines, service area, and FAQs. The AI answers routine questions conversationally, collects key qualifying criteria (such as project timeline and service requested), and offers available appointment slots. If a lead needs direct human assistance, the system alerts your team.

How does appointment booking integrate with my current calendar?

Our booking system connects directly with Google Calendar and Outlook in real time. Prospects only see open slots that fit your availability, and appointments appear instantly on your calendar with automated SMS and email reminders sent to reduce no-shows.

Can this connect with my existing website?

Yes. We integrate easily with WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, Shopify, or custom websites. We provide simple embeds for AI chat widgets, lead capture forms, and booking modules, or we can connect with your existing contact forms via webhooks.

Are there long-term contracts or setup penalties?

No long-term contracts. The $299/month plan is month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime. Our priority is providing a system that delivers clear value to your business every month.
